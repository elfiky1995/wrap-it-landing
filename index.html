Your files stay on your phone.

Wrap It has no server of its own. Converting, scanning, compressing and transcribing happen on the device, so there is nothing to upload and no account to create.

  • No sign-up, no login - the app opens straight to your tools.
  • Speech is transcribed by a model that runs on the phone.
  • Most tools keep working with no connection at all.

The three times it goes online

  • Downloading a video The app fetches it from the link you paste.
  • Translating a scan Translation of scanned text needs a connection.
  • Your first transcription A one-time 181 MB speech model download. The app asks before it starts.

Install in three steps

Wrap It is not on the Play Store. You download the app file from this page and Android installs it.

  1. wrap-it.apk 165 MB

    Download the file

    Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download. It is 165 MB, so Wi-Fi is the better choice.

  2. Allow the install

    Android asks once whether your browser may install apps. It shows this for every app that comes from outside the Play Store.

  3. Open Wrap It

    There is nothing to set up. Your tools are waiting on the home screen.
